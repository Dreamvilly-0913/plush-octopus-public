Illustrative scale: 1 sim unit = 10 cm (an octopus 26 cm tip to tip), stuffed like a minky shell at 0.05 g/cm³. Volume and energy are summed live from the stuffing cluster and the rod particles.

Inside the experiment

Stuffing. The head is a shape-matched cluster of 43 particles; each substep it is pulled back toward its rest shape by the stuffing amount, and its volume is read from the fitted deformation.

Arms. Eight Cosserat rods of 15 segments. Stretch–shear and bend–twist constraints hold a rest curl at every tip, so they hang, stretch and roll like stitched fabric tubes.

Fur. Shell-rendered pile. Comb strokes are painted into a nap map that leans the fibres; against the grain the pile stands and darkens.

Step. 10 substeps per frame, position-based. Floor friction, self-contact and finger contact are resolved inside every substep.
